About the app

LostGuitar is a full-featured Android music app with a black-and-dark-red, guitar-soaked identity — fronted by a black cat who shreds a red electric guitar. It's not a streaming-service clone; it's its own thing: a place to play, collect, and make music.

Everything runs on your phone. Stream the bundled original catalog, save it for offline listening, import your own MP3s, and build tracks from scratch in the built-in studio. All audio is original and royalty-free, made for LostGuitar.

Features

Offline DownloadsSave tracks to play with no connection.
MP3 UploaderImport your own audio into your library.
Music StudioRecord vocals, mix samples, arrange a timeline.
Drum Machine8 pads and a 16-step sequencer with BPM and metronome.
Sample LibraryDrums, bass, keys, synths, and FX baked in.
Projects & ExportAutosave projects and export your mix to WAV.
Music IdentificationTap a song and match it against the catalog.
Profiles & ThemesCustom banner, profile rings, and color themes.
